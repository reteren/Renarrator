Renarrator mixes trigger sounds into your microphone in real time (Soundpad-style). Pick your mic above. A virtual cable (e.g. VB-Audio Virtual Cable) is auto-detected here if installed — that's what other apps see as your mic, so pick its recording side ("CABLE Output") as your microphone in Discord/your game. On each trigger: "Play into microphone" sends it to others, "Play for myself" (on by default) plays it locally too.
